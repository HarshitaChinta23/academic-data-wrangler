# Academic Data Wrangler Using Python

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to identify business trends, product performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and revenue patterns using Python. It involves data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business analysis, and data visualization to generate meaningful insights.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-selling and most profitable products.
- Compare product categories based on revenue and profit.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Analyze monthly, yearly, and country-wise sales trends.
- Generate meaningful business insights.

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset Information
- **Dataset:** Sales Data
- **Total Records:** 113,036
- **Total Columns:** 18
- **Format:** CSV

The dataset includes information about dates, customers, products, categories, countries, order quantities, costs, revenue, and profit.

## Project Workflow

1. Data Collection and Loading
2. Data Exploration
3. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
4. Feature Engineering
5. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
6. Business Analysis
7. Data Visualization
8. Key Insights and Conclusions

## Key Analysis Performed
- Revenue analysis by product and category.
- Product-wise quantity and profit analysis.
- Profit margin calculation.
- Monthly and yearly revenue trends.
- Customer analysis by age group and gender.
- Country-wise revenue analysis.
- Top 10 countries based on revenue.
- Product profitability comparison.

## Key Insights
- Bikes contributed the highest share of total revenue.
- Accessories accounted for the highest sales quantity.
- Accessories had the highest profit margin among product categories.
- Revenue increased from 2011 to 2015 and declined in 2016.
- December and June recorded higher sales.
- Adult customers contributed the largest share of revenue.
- The United States and Australia generated a significant share of revenue.
